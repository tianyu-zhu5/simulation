## C. 5x5 contact variable definitions (authoritative)
- Contact pressure: solid.dcnt1.Tn (not solid.cnt1.Tn).
- Ac definition: integral of if(solid.dcnt1.Tn>0,1,0) over pc.destination.
- pn_avg definition: (int solid.dcnt1.Tn dA) / Ac.

## D. Delta scan strategy (why)
- Do not directly sweep 0.05-0.3 um, because that range can remain in the no-contact regime.
- Use delta_total = delta_base + delta_indent so contact is engaged before the indentation sweep.
- delta_base = gap0 + 0.05 um; delta_indent list = [0.05 0.1 0.15 0.2 0.22 0.25 0.3] um.
